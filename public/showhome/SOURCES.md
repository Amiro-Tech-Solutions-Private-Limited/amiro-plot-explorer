# North Park show-home assets

Visual reference: Genurise Builders' Living-Dining, Master-Bedroom and Master-Bathroom
visualisations at https://genurisebuilders.com/mjr-northpark-2-and-3-bhk-apartments-in-bagalur-bangalore/.
The developer's images retain their original copyright. They are references, not recovered
3D geometry, measured construction drawings, or a verified capture of an individual flat.

`northpark-furnishings.glb` is original geometry authored in
`scripts/build-showhome-models.mjs`: upholstered seating, bedding, drapery and sanitary ware.
Rebuild from the repository root with `node scripts/build-showhome-models.mjs`.
